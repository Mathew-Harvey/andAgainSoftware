Games

One out now, two on the way.

01Cover story

A first person view drone simulator, drawn like a manga.

73 lessons take you from a first hover to a five lap championship. WebFPV runs the open source flight controller firmware that racing drones fly on. It's free in your browser now. The Steam edition, for Windows, has lower latency and works offline. It comes out on 27 November for USD 9.99.

See the WebFPV page Play free in your browser

02Coming soon

Remote Pilot Academy

A drone career game.

Photograph houses, survey farms and inspect towers. Then hire a crew, grow the company and work up to light shows flown by a thousand drones. As you play, you learn the real drone rules for Australia, the United States and Canada. It's a game, not a licence. A web demo is coming soon.

See the game's page

03Coming to Steam

Ore Else

A first person strategy game.

Your first unit is a worker with a drill. Build a base and an army, then jump into any unit you own. It's coming to Steam, with a demo to play in your browser.

See the game's page

Apps and training

All three out now.

04Out now

The Bodyweight Gym

Learn the handstand. Then the ring muscle up.

57 full length video classes from the gym in Perth, all free to watch. My two books each teach one skill over six levels.

Watch the classes

05Out now

AppHub

One place for your team's small tools.

People build small apps with AI: a timer, a calculator, a notes page. Then they get lost in email attachments. Upload one and your team can run it. AppHub keeps every version and the prompt that made it.

Visit AppHub

06Out now

WebColoring

Free colouring pages for kids and grown-ups.

Nine books hold 270 pictures of dragons, block towns, mandalas and machines. Tap to fill, paint by hand or colour by numbers, on a phone, tablet or computer. It has no ads and no accounts, and it works offline.

Start colouring

Every picture comes from one of my colouring books. Some are out in paperback on Amazon.

About

Who makes this?

andAgainSoftware is me, Mat Harvey, in Perth, Western Australia. I make the games and tools I wanted for myself, and websites for a club and a client.

WebFPV is the simulator I wanted to learn on. AppHub is where my own small tools live now.

Nobody gets it on the first try. So you go again. That's the name.

Mat
