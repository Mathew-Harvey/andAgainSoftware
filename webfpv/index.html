The firmware is real.

Firmware is the code inside a racing drone that turns your sticks into motor speeds. WebFPV runs the real thing. It's the open source flight controller firmware that racing quads fly on, wired to a physics model of each quad.

1,000times a second, the firmware reads the gyro and sets the motors
73lessons in 16 chapters
16race tracks, for two quads
60+named tricks, graded clean or sloppy

Fly with a keyboard, a gamepad or an RC radio in USB joystick mode, in either edition. The Steam edition has the lower latency.

Four modes

Learn, race, freestyle and build.

01Learn

Lesson one: hover for five seconds.

Each lesson is one skill, with a briefing, a coach in your ear and a medal to chase.

You start in Angle mode, which levels the quad for you. By chapter three the levelling is off and you're in Acro, the mode racers fly. You finish by racing a five lap championship.

02Race

16 tracks, two quads.

The five inch quad has five race tracks. The whoop has three living rooms and eight Ultra Tiny Tracks. You get lap times, splits and a ghost of your best run.

The town
3D mode

03Freestyle

A town, and more than 60 named tricks.

As you fly, the game names each trick and grades it clean or sloppy.

Turn on 3D mode and the motors spin both ways, so you can hover upside down.

04Build

Race tracks, whoop rooms and freestyle maps.

Place the gates from above and fly the map straight from the builder.

Export a map as a file for a friend. On Steam, your maps also save to Steam Cloud.
